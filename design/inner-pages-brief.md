### Medicare rebates & fees (Fees & Rebates page)
I aim to keep the billing process as simple and transparent as possible:
- **Medicare Rebates:** If you have a Mental Health Care Plan (MHCP) or ED Plan from your GP, you will be eligible for a Medicare rebate per session.
- **How it Works:** You pay the session fee on the day, and the rebate is processed directly back into your bank account via Medicare—usually within 3 days.
- **Easy Payments:** All payments are securely processed at the end of your session using digital payment options.
- **No Complex Forms:** You don't need to fill out any compliance paperwork online. We will sort out all the details together during our initial phone call or first session.

**Payment methods:** Bank transfer · Electronic Funds Transfer · Mastercard · Visa

**Changing Your Appointment:** Your session time is reserved exclusively for you. If you need to cancel or change your appointment, please provide at least **2 full business days' notice**. Regrettably, cancellations made within this 2-business-day window or on the day of the appointment will incur a cancellation fee.

**Private Health Insurance:** Once your invoice is settled, we will issue a formal receipt so you can claim your private health insurance rebate. (Note: Health funds generally do not cover cancellation fees).

### DVA & Open Arms (pop-up)
- **Accepted DVA Cards:** We accept **Gold** and **White** Veteran Cards, including treatment provided under **Non-Liability Health Care (NLHC)** arrangements (which covers all mental health conditions for eligible current or former ADF members who have served at least one day).
- **No-Gap / Bulk Billing Policy:** We bill DVA directly with **no out-of-pocket gap fees or co-payments** for eligible services.
- **Referral Requirements:** We require a referral from your General Practitioner (GP), psychiatrist, or medical specialist.
- **Provider Registration Status:** I am registered with Medicare under the **Better Access** scheme as a psychologist (which is a statutory requirement for DVA allied mental health services).
- **Open Arms Support:** Open Arms – Veterans & Families Counselling, 1800 011 046, as an additional or after-hours resource.

### Body mapping (Services, arts-based therapy)
A Body map is a life-sized outline of the body filled with symbols, colour and texture that creatively express emotions and physical sensations attached to memories. The map is a tool to communicate complex experiences and support healing and growth.

My practice operates from an embodiment framework. This means we recognise that prolonged stress, depression, anxiety, anger, grief, shame, problems with managing emotions, emotional pain and childhood trauma, are not just stored as thoughts in the mind—they are physically held and expressed within the body itself. The effects of this can manifest as a deep sense of physical numbness or dissociation from your body, an internal feeling of tension and anxiousness from being unsafe, or chronic anxiousness and depression. We can sometimes use food control as an objective way to manage overwhelming bodily sensations.

By focusing on embodiment through tools like body mapping, we don't just talk about the symptoms. We work safely and creatively to understand the story your body is holding, helping you to bridge the gap between mind and physical self and support evidence-based therapies that are the mainstay of treatment.
