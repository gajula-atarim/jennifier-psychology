The practice

About the Practice

The clinic is located in the heart of Mooloolaba, where I offer a high-quality, personalised approach to mental health care. You will receive active, one-to-one care from an experienced psychologist dedicated to assessing and treating a range of mental health concerns, including PTSD, depression, anxiety, personality dynamics, and eating disorders.

I treat adults from all walks of life. I have also published research on how early life experiences affect our experience of the body.

My goal is to assist you to make sense of the origins of your difficulties, helping you build practical skills and make the changes needed to work towards a more meaningful life.

“I aim to look beyond symptoms, asking holistic questions to understand your whole story.”

How I work

About My Approach

I use a range of evidence-based therapies to support clients on their journey towards healing and personal growth. My approach incorporates Schema Therapy, Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), and Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT). I aim to look beyond symptoms, asking holistic questions to understand your whole story and help you truly reclaim your life.

  1. 01Schema TherapyPatterns, origins and root emotional wounds
  2. 02Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)Thoughts, feelings and behaviours
  3. 03Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT)Mindfulness, emotional regulation and distress tolerance
  4. 04Body mappingArts-based, embodied exploration of the self

To enrich the therapeutic process, you may choose to integrate arts-based techniques into your sessions. Body mapping is used to explore and express aspects of the self, supporting your therapy goals and fostering deep healing.

Telehealth

Telehealth allows clients to access psychological support from the comfort and privacy of their own home, reducing travel time and increasing accessibility for individuals with busy schedules, health concerns, caregiving responsibilities, or those living in rural and regional areas.

Your next step

What Next

In our initial sessions, we will work collaboratively to understand your unique needs and establish clear, meaningful treatment goals. Together, we will map out a personalised treatment plan—drawing on evidence-based therapies and embodied insights—to serve as a clear roadmap guiding our way forward on your journey to healing.

Your therapeutic timeline is flexible and uniquely tailored to your pace and clinical needs. If you are accessing care under a Medicare Mental Health Care Plan, we will structure our initial timing around your allocated subsidised sessions, regularly reviewing your goals together to ensure your plan continues to support your ongoing healing.
