Changing Your Appointment

Your session time is reserved exclusively for you. If you need to cancel or change your appointment, please provide at least 2 full business days' notice. Regrettably, cancellations made within this 2-business-day window or on the day of the appointment will incur a cancellation fee.

Medicare Rebates (Mental Health Care Plans)

To claim a Medicare rebate, you will need a valid Mental Health Care Plan (MHCP) and a referral letter from your GP or psychiatrist prior to your first session.

Under this plan, eligible clients can receive a Medicare rebate for up to 10 individual psychology sessions per calendar year.

Please note: The rebate will cover a portion of the session cost, leaving a residual out-of-pocket gap fee.

DVA (Department of Veterans' Affairs)

As a registered provider, I offer psychological support to eligible veterans and their families through the DVA framework.

If you hold a Gold Card or an approved White Card for mental health conditions, your consultations may be fully covered with no out-of-pocket expenses, provided you have a valid D904 referral from your GP.

NDIS Participants (National Disability Insurance Scheme)

I welcome clients who participate in the NDIS.

Services can be provided to participants whose funding is Self-Managed or Plan-Managed.

Sessions are focused on helping you build capacity, manage emotional distress, and work toward the personal goals outlined in your NDIS plan.
