Welcome

Personalised care in the heart of Mooloolaba

If you are living with Post Traumatic Stress Disorder or Complex PTSD, depression, anxiety, intense emotional distress or an eating disorder, help is here.

The clinic is located in the heart of Mooloolaba, where I offer a high-quality, personalised approach to mental health care. You can expect more one-to-one active attention from an experienced psychologist who will assess and treat your mental health concerns. I treat adults from all walks of life.

I also have published research, read globally, on how early life experiences and trauma affect our experience of the body.

In our very first session, we will work together to build a safe, collaborative plan to help you heal and reclaim your life.

Beyond symptoms

What we see is only part of the story

Anxiety, low mood or overwhelming emotions are often what bring people to therapy. They are real and they matter, but they are rarely the whole picture.

Approaches such as schema therapy help us gently explore what sits underneath, like early experiences and long-standing patterns, so we can work on causes as well as symptoms.
